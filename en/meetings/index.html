How the group decides

How meetings end, what a mistake costs and where meetings happen most.

The fewer alive, the more often someone is ejected

Meetings are grouped by how many players were alive at the start. The gold line is the share of meetings where someone was ejected, the red one is the share of those ejections that hit a goose. Whiskers show how far the share could drift with this many meetings.

someone ejected (of all meetings)of those, a goose (of all ejections)

Jail, ties and skips do not count as ejections.

Takeaway. Late in the game the group ejects more often, but not noticeably more accurately.

A first miss is not the end

Games are split by who was ejected first and what happened next. The dot is the share of those games the geese won.

“Duck” here means anyone but a goose: a duck or a neutral. Games where the Dodo was ejected (40) are left out: ejecting the Dodo ends the game.

Takeaway. After a miss, one thing decides it: whether a duck gets caught later. If it does, the geese win 71% of games; if not, 21%.

Who gets ejected

Which roles get ejected more often, how the group reads particular players, and who does not live to the first meeting.

Which roles get ejected more often

Ejections per hundred meetings the role was alive for.

Ducks and neutrals

The dot is ejections per hundred meetings alive; whiskers show how far it could drift.

Geese

The dot is ejections per hundred meetings alive; whiskers show how far it could drift.

Takeaway. The quietest duck is the Carrier: it kills nobody and gives nothing away, so it is almost never ejected (0.5 per hundred meetings).
Takeaway. Among geese, the ones with a knife (Vigilante, Sheriff) are wrongly ejected most: 1.3 per hundred meetings against 0.7 for other geese. They shoot, and draw suspicion onto themselves.

Reputation: how the group reads a player

Further right: the player is ejected more often as a duck; higher up: more often as a goose. Grey dots are players with up to half the threshold (30 duck and 60 goose games): they may still move a lot.

Takeaway. FANCY is the stealthiest duck: ejected 3.1 times less often than expected. LEMONВалера is ejected more than expected in both roles, as a duck and as a goose.
